Privacy notice

RunCoach is a technical demonstration, built as a recruitment exercise. It is not a commercial service and should not be used as one. This describes exactly what is kept, where, and for how long, because nobody should have to guess.

You are talking to a machine

The coach is not a person. It is a language model (Gemini, by Google) given a persona and training rules written by the author. It can be wrong, and it is sometimes confidently wrong. Its plans are guidance, not a substitute for a coach, a doctor or a nutrition professional.

What is kept

  • What you type and what you say. The text of the conversation and the transcript of anything spoken, so the coach remembers between sessions.
  • What the coach works out about you: goal, level, weekly kilometres and race date. These come out of the conversation, not from a form.
  • A random identifier your browser keeps in localStorage. There is no account, no email and no password. Clear it and you are a stranger again.
  • Your Telegram id and first name, only if you press "Conectar Telegram". The name is never asked for: Telegram sends it with every message, and it is stored so the coach can address you by it.
  • When you were last here, which is what the inactivity reminder is measured from, and which channel each message arrived on.
  • The time you asked to be reminded at, if you asked.

What is not kept

  • The audio. Your voice passes through the server on its way to the model and is discarded; it is never written to disk. What remains is the transcript.
  • Surname, email, phone or address. The only thing resembling a name is the first name Telegram provides, and only if you connect it.
  • Location, contacts, or anything else from your device beyond the microphone while you are actually using it.
  • No tracking cookies, no analytics, no third-party pixels. None.
  • Your full IP address. The web server notes every request, as they all do, but the last group is dropped before the line is written: what is stored looks like 187.147.235.0, which is enough to debug and not enough to identify anyone.

Who else sees it

Two third parties, and only because the product does not exist without them:

  • Google receives what you say, because it is what generates the reply. Its own terms for the Gemini API apply.
  • Telegram, only if you connect your chat, and only the messages on that channel.

Nobody else. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with advertisers, and nothing is used to profile anyone outside this application.

Where it lives

In a SQLite database on a single AWS server, encrypted in transit with TLS. The file is not readable by other users of that machine.

How long

As long as the demonstration is up. When the recruitment process ends the server is shut down and the data goes with it. There are no backups anywhere else.

Deleting yours

Ask the coach to delete your conversation, or write to the author at the address below. Clearing your browser's localStorage detaches you from the conversation, though it does not remove it from the server by itself.

The warning that matters more than the rest

The session identifier is not authenticated. That is a deliberate choice for a demonstration with no sign-up, and it means anyone who knew your identifier could read your conversation. Do not say anything here you would not want read.

And if you are going through something difficult, this is not the place for it. Talk to someone: in Mexico, Línea de la Vida answers on 800 911 2000, and in an emergency, 911. Elsewhere, your local emergency number.
